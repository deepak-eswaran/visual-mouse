HOLOHAND GUIDE

Explore with your hand

Enable hand mode, allow camera permission, then keep one hand visible in front of your camera.

  1. Point: your index fingertip moves the floating pointer around the page.
  2. Pinch: touch your thumb and index fingertips together. Pinch and move over the 3D object to rotate it; pinch and release over a control to select it.
  3. Zoom: raise your index and middle fingers while keeping ring and little fingers folded. Move your hand up to zoom in and down to zoom out.
  4. Switch object: open your palm and swipe horizontally to move through the collection.

For best tracking, face the camera, keep your hand well lit, and stay about an arm's length away.
